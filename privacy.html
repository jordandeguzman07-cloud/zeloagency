Australian Privacy & Data Handling

Privacy Policy

We respect your discretion and privacy. This policy outlines what information Zelo Escorts collects, how it is processed and stored, how retention and removal work, and how to request deletion.

1. Data Collection

When you submit a provider application or contact Zelo Escorts, we collect the details you provide for curation review and listing management:

  • Name & Contact Details: Your name or stage name, phone number, and email address.
  • Photos & Gallery Images: Uploaded portfolio photos and gallery images submitted for review and profile display.
  • Profile Information: City/location, listing tier, physical/appearance details, availability/service preferences, rates and notes, services offered, and bio.
  • Application Metadata: Reference IDs, timestamps, submission status, and structured submission records used to track review progress.

2. Processing & Automation

Submitted information may be processed through automation systems including Cloudflare Workers, Cloudflare R2, Google Sheets CRM, and Resend Email for operational purposes:

  • Cloudflare Workers: Receives and validates application submissions, generates reference metadata, and coordinates onboarding workflows.
  • Google Sheets CRM: Logs structured application fields, reference IDs, dates, and curation status for provider management.
  • Resend Email: Delivers application confirmation receipts and administrative notifications via admin@zeloescorts.com.au.
  • Analytics: High-level site traffic metrics are measured through Google Analytics (G-KB6ZGW8ZF3).

We do not sell personal contact details or application submissions to third-party data brokers.

3. Storage (Cloudflare R2)

Application media, supporting documents, and structured submission records may be stored within secure Cloudflare R2 cloud infrastructure to support curation review, archival integrity, and profile publishing.

4. Data Retention & Removal

Zelo Escorts retains applicant information for operational review purposes only. Upon successful publication of an approved profile, original application email copies and uploaded source media may be removed from active processing systems where no longer required for operational, security, legal, or administrative purposes.

Only information necessary to maintain a live profile, provide support, fulfill platform obligations, or maintain business records is retained.

5. Contact & Deletion Requests

Applicants may request deletion of their information by contacting:

admin@zeloescorts.com.au

When requesting deletion, please include your stage name, the email address or phone number used in your submission, and your Reference ID (if available) so we can locate and process your request promptly.
