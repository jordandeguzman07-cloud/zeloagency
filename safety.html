Safety & Resources Hub

Zelo Talent Agency is deeply committed to prioritizing the safety, privacy, and well-being of all independent providers and clients utilizing our advertising platform.

⚠️ Core Principle: Safety above all else. Independent operators maintain full control over their appointments, screening protocols, and personal boundaries at all times.

1. Screening & Verification Best Practices

Because independent providers operate as sole traders managing their own schedules, robust screening is your strongest layer of protection:

  • Verify Client Contact: Screen inquiries thoroughly. Ensure phone numbers are active and check industry reference networks where available before confirming appointments.
  • Trust Your Instincts: If a client communication feels evasive, aggressive, or raises red flags, always trust your instincts and decline the engagement.
  • Clear Boundaries: Establish your professional parameters, communication rules, and service guidelines upfront before any meeting takes place.

2. Appointment & Check-In Protocols

Physical safety requires reliable habits. We strongly encourage all independent providers to implement strict check-in routines:

  • Share Details: Always share appointment times, locations, and client contact details with a trusted friend, family member, or colleague.
  • Automated Check-Ins: Use check-in apps or set a designated time window to message your support contact before and after an engagement.
  • Control Your Environment: Meet in secure, pre-screened environments and ensure you have easy access to exits and communication devices.

3. Professional Industry Support & Advocacy

We believe in connecting providers with established Australian advocacy and support networks that champion health, legal rights, and workplace safety:

  • Scarlet Alliance: The Australian Sex Workers Association, providing comprehensive health, legal, and safety toolkits nationwide. (scarletalliance.org.au)
  • Local Health & Legal Services: Access state-based health services and legal aid networks for confidential advice and support.

4. Platform Structure & Independence

Zelo Agency acts solely as an independent digital advertising and search optimization directory. We do not operate an agency booking desk or manage dispatch services. All profiles featured represent independent sole traders managing their own businesses legally under Australian state guidelines.
